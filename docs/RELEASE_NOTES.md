Filter the Found panel by conductor, composer, year or anything else in the title.

**Download the file for your computer below:**

- Windows: `browseDL-windows-setup.exe` (installer) or `browseDL-windows-portable.exe` (no install)
- Mac (Apple silicon): `browseDL-macos-arm64.dmg`. Before the first launch, run `xattr -cr /Applications/browseDL.app` in Terminal, since the app isn't signed by Apple.
- Linux (x86-64): `browseDL-linux.AppImage`

### New: filter box

At the top of the Found panel, type to narrow the list, e.g. `rattle`, `simon mahler` or `petrenko 2019`.

- Every word has to appear somewhere in the title or the text around it (dates included), and accents don't matter: `dvorak` finds *Dvořák*, `olafsson` finds *Ólafsson*.
- **Tick all** ticks only what the filter shows.
- Ticks are kept when you change the search, so you can search for one conductor, tick all, then search for another and tick those too. The panel says when ticked items are hidden by the current search, with a button to untick them.
- Press Esc to clear it.

### Better

- Linked pages are titled by the concert's name instead of having "CONCERT" tacked on, and their date shows underneath (and can be searched).
- Single-quality pieces of a stream (named like `index-f6-v1-a1`) are no longer listed next to the stream itself.
